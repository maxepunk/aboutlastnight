## C4. About 1,500 words, every paragraph pulling the reader on

The article runs to about 1,500 words, and the thesis decides what earns space. Every paragraph and card pulls the reader into the next, and every sharp line makes a claim about this session. Recaps, scaffolding, lines that announce what is coming and lines that could sit in any article are cut. The players know how the game works because they played it. Where the record stops on something that matters, Nova says so in one plain line and lets it lead into the suspicion or the question it raises, and explains no more of the game than that. House style puts a comma, a colon or a full stop where an em-dash might go.

Why: players stop reading long articles and flat paragraphs, and a game explained to the people who played it is a paragraph they skip.
