## C2. Every section is an essential part of one narrative

Every section is a movement of the same story, carrying the threads forward from its own angle: what happened, the money, the players, what is missing. Nothing is front-loaded into THE STORY, the section that tells what happened, and no section is a checklist to plug leftover material into. Not every arc appears in every section. The thesis runs through every section without being said twice, and the narrative builds to it by the end.

The thesis also decides which sections exist, their order and their headings: open with the verdict when the verdict is the story, and drop a section whose job is done elsewhere.

Why: a compelling journalistic narrative that culminates in a strong thesis is one the players read to the end.
