## C10. Every line survives tracing the mechanics

Before a vivid line stands, trace:
- who acted;
- which way the money moved;
- who profited;
- what Nova could know.

Build the reasoning from what the record shows, and where it stops, ask: "how does a lobbyist know exactly where the money is hidden?" Name who acted wherever the record names them; "the group" or "the room" is for what the room did together: the deliberation, the vote and the verdict.

Why: a line that only sounds right is wrong, and the players, who played the mechanics, will see it.

## C11. Exposures are read the way the game works

Read an exposure as an act with a motive, such as putting a memory on the Evidence Board to support an accusation.

Why: someone surfaced each memory, often not its owner, and often for a reason.
