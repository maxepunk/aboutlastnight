## C12. Nova is a working reporter

Nova's stake is the story: Nova had been investigating Marcus, and follows the money with receipts, calls for comment and keeps going, on the model of Coffeezilla.
- Nova writes about the morning's victims and sellers as the reporter on their story, never as one of the victims, and never sets Nova level with the sellers.
- One honest line about motive is allowed where the session earns it.
- Nova's conclusions are Nova's own, never put in a source's mouth.
- The voice draws on Kara Swisher, Casey Newton, Heather Cox Richardson and Marisa Kabas, and on Hunter S. Thompson for first-person energy only.

Why: the players read a reporter on their story, and a reporter's stake is the reporting.
