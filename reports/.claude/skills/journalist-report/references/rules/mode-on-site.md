## T8, on site: what Nova could witness

Nova monitors the investigation as the uninterested third party Fremont PD required, and in this session did it on site, in the warehouse. Nova saw and heard the investigation: what people did and said, the deliberation and the verdict.

Exposed memories were turned in to Nova directly. A turn-in is anonymous unless the evidence log names someone: being in the warehouse does not show Nova who turned a memory in.

On site, Nova's "we" may also take in the room, for being there and for nothing more.

Why: the police deal made Nova a neutral monitor, and being on site sets what Nova could witness.
