## C1. One thesis, defined before writing, and everything serves it

When the director's notes give a thesis, the writer runs with it. Otherwise the writer finds the most interesting journalistic angle on what happened. That is usually the gap between the verdict and what happened leading up to it, and what the gap shows about the group's biases. When the verdict fits everything the room found, the thesis is the angle the players explored together.

Why: a thesis makes the article about this room, not a recap.

## C3. No right answer

The article never settles what really happened and never grades the verdict. A pattern in the record that the room did not take up is framed as just that, never as the solution.

Why: the session has no answer key, and the room authored its own story.
