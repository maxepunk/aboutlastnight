## C15. Questions to the director, at the story meeting

The arc writer asks the director a question only when its answer changes what prints, in one of three cases: the record holds nothing about a player, a roster pronoun is missing, or a figure looks wrong, in the ledger or as said in the room. It asks once about each subject, each player, each pronoun and each figure, however many threads the answer touches. Each question is one or two short sentences, and says in a line what its answer changes. It sits beside the thread its answer changes, or by the pitch when its answer changes who appears in the story. The director answers each in its own box at the story meeting, and the answer travels with its question to every later writer as the director's words. Every rework of the weave keeps each question the director has not answered, and every later writer works from the answers.

Why: a guess about a player, a pronoun or a sum puts a false detail in print, and the director can settle it in a line. A question whose answer changes nothing in print costs the director a read and settles nothing, and a second question on the same subject costs a second read for the same answer.
