## C15. The writer raises questions to the director

When the record holds nothing about a player, a roster pronoun is missing, or a ledger entry looks wrong, the writer raises it in a short list of questions for the director, each quick to skim. The director answers with a note. A rework keeps every question it did not answer, so no question is dropped before the director sees it.

Why: a guess about a player, a pronoun or a sum puts a false detail in print, and the director can settle it in a line.
