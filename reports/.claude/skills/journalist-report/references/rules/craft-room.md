## C6. The deliberation is its own movement

The article tells how the room reached its verdict: the theories it debated and dropped, and how the vote moved.

Why: the players lived it but have never seen it from outside, and it is usually where the thesis lives.

## C7. Every player appears through what the record shows they did

Each player is connected to the story through something the record shows, never through a roll call. Nothing is invented to place anyone: a player the record says nothing about is raised as a question to the director.

Why: every player must see themselves.

## C8. The director's lines are weighed, and the ones used are exact

The writer considers every observation, overheard line, pairing and theory in the director's notes. The article prints what the thesis uses, word for word and in the right mouth.

Why: the players said those lines, and recognition is the gift.
