# The director's words

Everything the director gives the pipeline as record: the session config inputs, the accusation, the input-review corrections, the photo descriptions, the exposures, the ledger's shift, the whiteboard, and the enricher's indexes of the notes.

Moved verbatim from `reports/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-11, when CLAUDE.md became a map. A part named in bold elsewhere in this text, such as **The map**, may now sit in another file in `docs/architecture/`; CLAUDE.md lists which file holds which part.

**The director's words as record** (phase 2, brief 2.2; `lib/prompt-renderers/director-words-renderer.js` renders every section below, one function per section, and nothing in it paraphrases the director):
- **Accusation.** Parse step 1's `accusation` carries a required `verdictKind` (`lib/accusation-verdict.js` `VERDICT_KINDS`: `culprit`, `accident`, `overdose`, `self-harm`, `other`). Every kind but `culprit` names no one, and `normalizeAccusation` empties `accused` for it in code (on 092026 the model listed the victim for an accidental-overdose verdict). The director's accusation, word for word, is stamped by code as `sessionConfig.accusationRaw`, so it is stored with the parse in state and in `inputs/session-config.json`; `directorAccusationText(state)` falls back to the `accusation` channel for a parse from before the field. It reaches the weave writer, its rework and its fact check (`renderArcAccusation`) and the map and article writers' `SESSION_FACTS` (`renderSessionFactsVerdict`) as `<DIRECTOR_ACCUSATION>`, beside the parsed accused and the CHARGE. A no-culprit verdict prints `ACCUSATION: none (the room's verdict names no culprit: …)`. `SESSION_FACTS` is built once, by `buildSessionFacts` in `ai-nodes.js`. The enricher's `<ACCUSATION>` block now carries the parse's `notes`. After a verdict that places no character, the weave's accusation block adds one sentence (`noCulpritInstruction`): the thread that tells the verdict holds no one as the accused, and never the victim (brief 4.5, ruling 11). Phase 3 (brief 3.5): the session-config parse reads the director's text as THE GROUP STATEMENT (no longer "MURDER ACCUSATION") and names Marcus Blackwood as the man whose death the room investigates, never accused. A verdict that blames an institution or an unnamed person is a `culprit` verdict naming no character, with the room's words as the charge (`blamesNoCharacter`); it prints `ACCUSATION: no character (… see the charge)`, and the arc block says the thread that tells the verdict holds no character as the accused. Without a charge it is a failed parse, not that shape: the input review keeps its red "not parsed" line, and no writer is told to place no character. A split final vote keeps every option with its count and the one the statement adopted, or none (`accusation.votes`, cleaned by `normalizeVotes`, present only for a split); `buildSessionFacts` and `synthesizePlayerFocus` carry it, and `SESSION_FACTS` prints `FINAL VOTE (split): …` and the arc block `**Final Vote (split):** …` (`formatSplitVote`). With no charge, `playerFocus.primaryInvestigation` is the room's verdict on Marcus Blackwood's death, not "Who killed Marcus Blackwood?".
- **Input-review corrections.** Each send back is also appended to `inputReviewCorrections` (REPLACE channel, the director's text in order; `_inputCorrections` is still the per-round trigger the re-parse consumes). Every parse call gets the whole list (a re-parse starts again from the source text), the whiteboard parse included. The weave, map and article writers get it as `<DIRECTOR_CORRECTIONS>` right after `<DIRECTOR_NOTES>` (`renderDirectorEnrichmentBlock`'s `corrections`), labelled as the director's words that override the notes where they differ; the notes and `rawProse` are never rewritten. Cleared where the parse is redone (the three points that clear `accusation`), kept by a rollback to `input-review`.
- **Photo descriptions.** The character-IDs stop sends `photoDescriptions` (`{filename: text}`) beside `characterIdsRaw`; the console pairs each analysis with its photo by filename (`checkpoint-view-logic.js` `characterIdCards` / `characterIdsPayload`), not by array index. `buildResumePayload` keeps the text trimmed at the ends only and refuses a malformed map; `checkpointCharacterIds` captures it into the `photoDescriptions` channel and writes `inputs/photo-descriptions.json`. The map writer's `<available-photos>` and the article writer's PHOTOS print each photo as filename, identified names and the description word for word (`renderPhotoEntry`, joined by basename, case-insensitive; since 3.9 the article writer prints each entry once, under PHOTOS; the arc packages' ARC PHOTOS went with the packages in phase 4, brief 4.6, R5); "The director's description: none given" only when a map of descriptions exists and lacks that photo, and no description line at all when the thread has none (every thread from before this stop collected descriptions); Haiku's pre-identification descriptions no longer reach the map writer. On site only (phases 14 and 15, R4; spec 2026-10-09 section 5), Nova may be in a photo: the character-IDs parse prints Nova's names after VALID ROSTER and the enrichment after its `ROSTER:` line, under THE ARTICLE'S WRITER (`writerInPhotosLine`, from the theme's `writer.inPhotos`), so neither corrects Nova's name to a player's, and the photo entries (`heroPhotoEntry`, `buildAvailablePhotos`, through `photoNamesOf` and `markWriterInPhoto`) mark a name equal to Nova's first name as `Cassandra (Nova, who writes this article)`, and one that holds Nova's own name, "Nova" or the two together, with the theme's shorter mark, `Nova (who writes this article)`, so it is not repeated (`writer.inAPhoto` and `writer.inAPhotoByName`; the final fix wave, K4). The roster decides a clash: a name a roster player has, first or full (a full name's first word counts), stays the player's. Remote, the parse and the entries are as before. Cleared with `characterIdMappings`. **A kept photo** (T13: every photo the director has not excluded appears, and an excluded photo never does) is decided by one rule everywhere (the 4b fix batch): the director's own decision at the character-IDs stop (`characterIdMappings[<filename>].exclude`) first, the analysis's `excluded` flag as the fallback. Since phase 4 (brief 4.2) that decision is the stop's "leave this photo out" box on each photo (`lib/photo-leave-out.js`). The ticked filenames go to the `leftOutPhotos` channel: for every photo the stop showed the box decides, and a listed photo the stop did not show stays (`listAfterStopChoices`). In each of its paths `parseCharacterIds` then writes an explicit `exclude` into the mapping of every photo the stop showed: true when the photo is on the list, otherwise the parse's own value (an exclusion the director's text asked for), otherwise false (`withExplicitExclusions`). So the analysis flag, which a rollback to character-ids leaves stale, decides only for a photo the stop never showed. `finalizePhotoAnalyses` takes a stale flag off each photo it keeps (task 4.3c), so the analysis and `enrichmentStats.excluded` agree with the mapping; the flag stays stale only where it skips, once an earlier round has identified someone in a photo. `leavePhotosOut(state, filenames)` is the one function that adds to the list: it also sets `exclude: true` in each photo's mapping, creating the mapping when the photo has none, so an exclusion added after the parse holds. The list is cleared wherever `characterIdMappings` is. One predicate, `isPhotoExcluded` (`ai-nodes.js`), decides the map writer's `<available-photos>` and the photos the map checks hold it to (`keptPhotoFilenames`), code's pick for the top photo (`selectHeroImage` never picks an excluded photo, and with no kept photo it picks none, where it once fell back to a placeholder that was no session photo, so no photo is marked `[hero image]`), the article writer's and its judge's PHOTOS, and which photos the fact check's photo-reference check counts as usable. A photo's mapping is the one `photoMappingOf` (`lib/photo-leave-out.js`) finds, by `photoKey`, which `isPhotoExcluded`, `finalizePhotoAnalyses` and `withExplicitExclusions` all read (brief 4.2b). `heroPhotoEntry` and `renderPhotoListEntry` (`director-words-renderer.js`) build the hero entry and the numbered PHOTOS entries for the writers and judges alike.
- **Exposures.** Parse step 2 keeps each exposed memory's `exposer` ("Exposed By", the name on the turn-in to Nova), `time` and `owner` (`exposures` in `inputs/orchestrator-parsed.json`, stamped onto `sessionConfig.exposures`), and the input review lists them. Since phase 3 (brief 3.5) the investigation's timeline in the record view prints each one's time and the name on its turn-in ("anonymous" or "named: <name>"), only for memories the bundle holds as exposed; the owner column prints nowhere. The parse also stamps `sessionConfig.exposedTokenCount`, the length of the exposed-memory id list disposition reads (`exposedTokens`): the input review says in red that every memory will count as buried only when it is 0, and says separately when the per-row log is empty ("no exposure times or turn-in names"), through `checkpoint-view-logic.js` `exposuresView`.
- **The session report's adjustments** (phases 14 and 15, brief C; spec section 4, R3). The report's clock can run hours off the game's (on 100326 and 100426 it logged the bonus and the transfers nine hours ahead), so the parse lines them up before any writer reads them. `buildLedger` takes the exposures beside the sales, and `lineUpOffClockEvents` (`lib/session-ledger.js`) tests each classified event, the bonus or a transfer, against the session's span: its first sale or exposure to its last, in the session clock's first-event order. An event more than 180 minutes outside it (`OFF_CLOCK_MINUTES`) is off the clock; the setup row and an unclassified row make no event and stay as logged, and the bonus was paired with its holding-account debit on the logged minutes before. Every off-clock event moves by one whole-hour shift, -12 to +12, among those that land every one of them inside the span: when the bonus is among them, the one that lands it nearest the first sale, since the bonus is paid with it (a tie to the bonus at or after the sale, then the fewer hours); otherwise the fewest hours (a tie to the shift back). A moved event's `time` is the shifted time, written by `session-clock.js` `loggedTimeFromMinutes` ("03:55 PM"), and its `loggedTime` the time as logged; `ledgerCheck` gains `clockShift: {hours, moved, bonus}`, or `offClock: {rows}` with nothing moved when no shift fits, and neither key on a session whose events all sit within the margin (the repo's fixtures put a transfer two and a half hours after the last sale). The timeline, the evidence check and the writers read `sessionConfig.adjustments` and need no change. The input review says what moved (`shiftLine`; see the `ledger` row below). A thread parsed before this keeps its logged times; a new parse, or the input review's Reject with corrections, lines them up (R11), and a note at any later stop corrects a wrong shift for the writers and the judges.
- **Whiteboard.** `playerFocus.whiteboardContext` reaches the map and article writers inside `SESSION_FACTS` under the arc writer's label (`renderWhiteboardConnections`), left out when the whiteboard held nothing. Since phase 3 (brief 3.5) the label is "The Whiteboard (a model's reading of the photo)": context for how the room reasoned, not a source, never "Players drew these". The parse keeps each region under the heading the players wrote (`regions`: `{label, location, entries}`; an older parse's `groups` read as regions), matches names against the roster, every character and the NPCs (`buildWhiteboardPrompt`'s `characters` and `npcs`), keeps unsure text as written, and its ambiguities reach the writers. `synthesizePlayerFocus` reads the regions; the suspect list taken from the first group labelled "suspect" is gone.
- **Arc stop.** The arc writer and arc reworker end with the standing notes, through the same `buildDirectorGuidanceSection` + `filterGateNotes(…, 'arc-selection')` the map and article prompts use, so a second send back no longer loses the first note. The story meeting's note box is kept with the director's weave on every change, and both survive a remount of the same weave version (see **The story meeting on screen**).

**The director's notes, unguessed** (phase 3, task 3.6). The enricher's indexes carry the director's words as written, and the machine's readings are labelled as readings:
- **Quotes.** A quote's speaker may be left out. Its words come from the notes, or from the correction that gives the wording; `context` is the director's words around it, one unbroken passage copied from the notes (the quote's sentence through the sentence that names the speaker, with every sentence between), because only a passage the notes hold in one piece survives the check; `correction` is the input-review correction that changed its speaker, addressee or wording. `groundQuotes` (`lib/director-enricher.js`) keeps the words, the context and the correction only when the notes or the corrections hold them word for word (`isVerbatimIn`, `lib/grounding.js`, which the renderer shares), and keeps the speaker and the addressee only when the kept context or correction names them outside the quoted words (`namedOutsideQuote`, also in `lib/grounding.js`). It leaves both out, counted as `unrecordedSpeakers`, when the quote carries a correction the corrections do not hold. A verified correction is about the line when the corrections hold the attached one, or when one of them quotes the line (task 3.11: a passage the correction puts in quotation marks holds a whole sentence of three or more words of the quote's words or of the passage its context quotes, as `isVerbatimIn` reads them and on word boundaries; corrections quote names, vote words and charges as often as lines, and those sit inside a sentence or are shorter, so they never decide); since the final fix the speaker is then the name that correction gives and the notes' account of the line does not, attached or not, because a correction names the old speaker as well as the new, and with no such name both are left out. A quote whose speaker is left out has the confidence `low`. The confidence bands are one wording, `QUOTE_CONFIDENCE_BANDS`, in the schema and the rules (M5). The enricher's rules (`ENRICHMENT_RULES`: verbatim, roster, link, quote, epilogue, empty) are stated once per call, each with its reason, in `<ENRICHMENT_RULES>` after the notes in the user prompt (the corrections block, when there is one, follows it); the system prompt points there by the tag alone. The link rule joins an observation to a ledger row only when the notes describe that sale (someone seen selling, or a deal with Blake) and its time and amount converge; an account's name matching a character never makes a link (T4). `<QUOTE_BANK>` prints each quote with its speaker or `(speaker not recorded)` (a stored `unknown` too), then an `In the notes:` line and a `The director's correction:` line, each only when the renderer finds it verbatim; a stored speaker or addressee prints only under the rule the enricher uses, `groundQuote` (`lib/grounding.js`, the final fix): with no correction about the line, a name the verified context gives; with a verified correction about the line, attached or not, only a name that correction gives and the notes' account of the line (the context and the notes' paragraph holding it) does not, so a line the director re-attributed never stays in the old speaker's mouth; a correction's quoted passage matches in single or double quotation marks and in any case (task 3.11: a thread enriched before phase 3 (before 3.6's enricher) stores speakers with no support, such as 092626's "Mel" for a thought in Mel's memory, so its quotes print `(speaker not recorded)` wherever nothing verified names one); it no longer says "prefer these" or prints the confidence, and it has no em-dash.
- **The epilogue.** An item is `{detail, subjects?, proseOffset?}`: `detail` is one unbroken passage of the director's words (a development the notes report in two places is two items), kept only when the notes hold it (`groundEpilogue`), and `subjects` is an index no prompt prints. The enricher's headline and "why this matters" are gone. The renderer prints `<EPILOGUE>` (it was `<POST_INVESTIGATION_NEWS>`) with each item's verbatim `detail` alone, so a stored item with a headline prints as the director's sentence and one with only a headline prints nothing. The stored field keeps its name, `postInvestigationDevelopments`.
- **Labels.** `<TRANSACTION_LINKS>` carries `DERIVED_LABELS.transactionLinks` (a link is the model's pairing of an observation with ledger sales, never something the notes state; `groundLinkExcerpts` keeps a link only when the notes hold its observation word for word, so the label's "quoted from the director's notes" holds; the renderer re-checks each stored link the same way, `linksInTheNotes` in `director-notes-renderer.js`, and prints nothing of one whose observation the notes do not hold, so a thread enriched before that check reads the same) and `<EPILOGUE>` `DERIVED_LABELS.epilogue` (the model picked the sentences; each is copied as written). `<QUOTE_BANK>` keeps `directorNotesIndex`.
- **Character extraction** requires no role, lists a relationship only where a document states it and a group only where a document names the character a member, and gives Marcus and Blake by the canon lines (T15). That document-only rule is stated once, last in the prompt, with its reason; the schema and the system prompt only name the fields, and the field list asks for "each relationship a document states between them and another character", so the list itself asks for nothing inferred.
- **The tensions.** `surfaceContradictions` no longer reads an account named after a roster character as that character's (T4): the named-account and transparency tensions are gone. Its one tension prints the director's sentences that name Blake or the Valet, and `DERIVED_LABELS.narrativeTensions` says those sentences are record and only their gathering is code's (from wave 2 the print sites drop stored old-type tensions, so every printed tension is the director's sentences); `proseSentences` does not end a sentence at a full stop inside the director's quotation marks, and ends one at a blank line with LF or CRLF line endings.
- New `_enrichmentWarnings` keys: `droppedContexts`, `droppedCorrections`, `unrecordedSpeakers`, `droppedEpilogueItems`, `droppedExcerpts` (links dropped because the notes do not hold their observation). The input review shows `droppedQuotes`, and through `ENRICHMENT_WARNING_LABELS` in `console/input-review-logic.js` `unrecordedSpeakers`, `droppedContexts` and `droppedEpilogueItems` (since 3.5) and `droppedCorrections` and `droppedExcerpts` (since the 3.6b fix batch), each as a count with a short label.
