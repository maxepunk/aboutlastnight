# Old threads

Threads from before the story meeting, or on an earlier shape: how they are flagged, refused and rolled back.

Moved verbatim from `reports/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-11, when CLAUDE.md became a map. A part named in bold elsewhere in this text, such as **The map**, may now sit in another file in `docs/architecture/`; CLAUDE.md lists which file holds which part.

### Threads from before the story meeting (task 4.11; R2)

A thread started before phase 4 holds no weave, and its outline and article are in the old shapes. Once the old stages have taken it past the arc writer, the new stages would replay it on those shapes and spend paid calls the director never asked for. `lib/old-thread.js` flags it (`oldThreadOf(values, pausedAt)`, by the weave, ruling 1) when it holds no weave and is paused at the meeting's stop or a later one in `CHECKPOINT_ORDER` (`photos`, `character-ids`, the map or the article), is complete, or holds what only a stage past the arc writer writes (`PAST_THE_ARC_WRITER`): an evaluation, a rework's count, an outline or an article. The last covers a thread at no stop, one that stopped on an error or whose run was killed (fix rounds 1 and 2), wherever the old stages left it: the old arc stage evaluated its arcs before the arc stop opened, and every rework counted itself before it ran, so a thread that stopped in the photo branch, the old outline writer or the old outline rework (which emptied the outline first, and whose catch left it empty) is flagged as well as one that holds an outline or an article. Resumed, such a thread reached a fresh meeting and then ran the new stages on the old stages' work: the map's stop opened on the old outline, or on a fresh map with the old outline stage's note, edits, trace and counts, and the old arc stage's counts spent the fresh weave's check rework. A thread of the new code holds none of them without a weave: the arc writer writes the weave or throws, every evaluation and rework after it runs on the weave, the fresh start, every rollback that clears the weave and an old thread's rollback to the meeting clear them all, and the stub a rollback past the meeting writes into the evaluations (`source: 'rollback'`) is no evaluation, which `lib/__tests__/old-thread.test.js` holds. A thread with no weave that holds none of them is resumed, and its resume writes the weave: a new thread before its weave, an old one rolled back to the meeting before its weave is written, or an old one that stopped in its arc writer, before any check rework or evaluation (one that stopped in the old arc check's rework holds that rework's count, so it is flagged).

A thread on an earlier shape is old too: phase 4's (phase 4b, brief 1G; spec 2026-10-05 section 10; R9) and piece 1's (piece 3, brief 3E; spec 2026-10-06 section 13; R6). The story level made a thread a name and a line with its evidence, and a beat a move; piece 3 pitches the story as angles, where piece 1's weave told one story with threads in roles. Every new gate refuses the earlier shapes, and nothing converts them or reads piece 1's weave as one angle. `oldThreadOf` flags a thread whose weave is in an earlier shape (`isOldShapeWeave`: a weave with no `angles`, which covers piece 1's shape and phase 4's) or whose map is (`isOldShapeMap`: a beat, in a section or in left out, with no `move`), wherever it sits: at a stop, complete, or at no stop after an error, since only the arc writer writes a weave and only the map writer a map. Its message is its own and names neither shape, `OLD_SHAPES_MESSAGE`: "This session's story meeting was written in an earlier form. Roll back to the story meeting to write it again." Every rollback point above the meeting clears the weave and the map, so a thread paused before the meeting holds neither and is read as before. Both tests live in `lib/old-thread.js`, which `scripts/render-prompts.js` reads too.

`server.js` refuses an old thread: `/approve`, `/resume` (`force` included) and every `/rollback` past the meeting answer 409 `{sessionId, error, oldThread}`, the error the flag's message: "This session was started before the story meeting. Roll back to the story meeting to continue." (`OLD_THREAD_MESSAGE`) for a thread with no weave, `OLD_SHAPES_MESSAGE` for one on an earlier shape. A rollback to the meeting or a point before it proceeds, and writes the weave fresh with its fact check; the meeting's point also starts over the arc counters the old arc stage left (`oldThreadRollbackState(rollbackTo, values)`, whose `values`, the thread's state, are required: a call without them throws, naming the function, since it could not tell the earlier shapes; fix round 3), and on a thread on an earlier shape clears the weave's channels that point keeps under R9 (`OLD_SHAPES_WEAVE_CHANNELS`: the weave with its fact check's mark and the director's pick, `_weaveBaseline`, `_weaveHandEdits` and the weave checks' `_arcValidation`) beside the point's own list (`ROLLBACK_CLEARS['arc-selection']`: the meeting's approval, round, marks and report, the map and its channels, the article and the evaluations), so the weave writer writes the angles fresh, and the checks, the fact check and every stage after them run fresh. The director's notes and the photo stops' choices stay, as on any rollback to the meeting. `GET /checkpoint` carries the flag as `oldThread`, `{message, rollbackTo: 'arc-selection', rollbackPoints}` (null for every other thread), and sends an old thread's stop as `{type, oldThread}` alone, building no stop's payload from the old shapes. The console reads the flag through `oldThreadView` (`checkpoint-view-logic.js`): `app.js` shows the message and a "Roll back to the story meeting" button in place of the stop, whose component never renders, above a loaded completion (`completedResultFrom` carries the flag), and, for a thread at no stop, in place of the resume: the Session screen classifies it `old-thread` (`classifyCheckpointResponse`), loads the flag into the state (`OLD_THREAD_LOADED`, cleared by the rollback's checkpoint) and posts nothing. Each way the stepper opens only `rollbackPoints`. A refused request's 409 shows its message on the banner and is never taken for the session lock's. Tests: `lib/__tests__/old-thread.test.js` (the old-shape tests by stop and shape too), `__tests__/integration/old-thread-guard.test.js` (each stop and request, and threads at no stop, on either kind), `__tests__/integration/old-thread-graph.test.js` (the real graph: refused at `photos`, after a failed publish and after an error in the old outline rework, each then rolled back to the meeting, and a new thread whose weave writer failed, resumed; on phase 4's shapes and on piece 1's weave, refused at the meeting, at the map and complete, each rolled back to the meeting, where the weave is written fresh in the new shape, with its angles, beside a thread on the new shapes left alone) and `console/__tests__/checkpoint-view-logic-old-thread.test.js`. The old shapes in them are `lib/__tests__/fixtures/old-shapes.js`, invented text in phase 4's shapes and in piece 1's (`pieceOneMeetingChannels`, `pieceOneMapChannels`).
