# The map

Moved verbatim from `reports/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-11, when CLAUDE.md became a map. A part named in bold elsewhere in this text, such as **The map**, may now sit in another file in `docs/architecture/`; CLAUDE.md lists which file holds which part.

## The page

### The map on screen (`Outline.js`; task 4.9; piece 4, brief 4D)

The outline stop's label is "Map". The page is a corkboard (spec 2026-10-07 sections 4 to 7), `mapView(data, map)`'s on the map as the director has it, and `Outline.js` renders its parts in its `order` (`MAP_SECTIONS`), the order `lib/stop-pages.js` follows too, through one renderer per part (`PARTS`), deciding no line, label, count or tone itself:
- `round`, the round's lines, when there are any: a send-back whose rework did not run; after a send-back, the round and the note it carried; a check still failing that no line shows; each edit of the director's that `changedEditsToShow` keeps and no move, section, the top of the article, the gap note, the length or the weave changes shows, a send-back's with its reason and any other pass's that code did not put back (task 4.10), or, when it keeps none, that the director's edits stand (`kept`, from `editsStandLine`; brief 4.10b); a concern no line shows.
- `tally`, the counts in one row that wraps on a narrow screen (fix H6), under the aria-label "The counts" (`PAGE_REGIONS`): `placed`, "Everyone placed", or `unplaced`, "In no move: ...", and `raised`; `cards`, "4 cards", with the range when they leave it; `photos`, "10 of 10 photos"; `length`, "About 1,350 words", under its pencil, with its `lengthConcerns` and `changedBeside.length` under it, its editor open or not. The Everyone list went: the cards carry the people.
- `settledStory`, read-only, with a button back to the story meeting (the existing rollback, which reopens the meeting with no model call), and the gap note beside it with its editor, its `failures`, `concerns` and `changedBeside.gapNote` under it, its editor open or not; the last show there on a map that holds no gap note any more.
- `legend`, the threads, from the payload's `legend`: each story thread a chip with its dot (`tone`, its place 1 to `MAP_TONES` (8) and repeating) and its name (`label`); picking one (`labels.pick`) shows its `line` under the chips, outlines the cards that carry it (a card's `legendKeys`) and dims the rest, and picking it again, or `showAll`, clears it.
- `top`, the headline and the deck under their pencil, the top photo, or with none `top.noPhoto` (`MAP_NO_TOP_PHOTO_LINE`) in its place, saying the article prints no top photo and how to set one (fix H3), and `top.changed`, an edit a rework changed that is about the headline, the deck or the top photo (spec 7).
- `sections`, the board: the button that opens or closes every summary (`summaries`, shown when any card has one), then a column per section, side by side and stacked on a narrow screen. Each column is headed by its slot's label from the payload's `mapSlots` (code names no slot), its heading or `MAP_NO_HEADING_LINE`, and its job, under the head's pencil, with its `failures`, `concerns` and `changed` under the head, its editor open or not; then its cards in the order the article tells them (`beats`), its photos by themselves (`byThemselves`), `emptied` (`MAP_EMPTIED_COLUMN_LINE`) on a column the director emptied, and its add line ("+ Add a move").
- `leftOut`, the tray, always open (R8): each move by its title and its dots.
- `dropped`, each with its reason, a section the director emptied read as "You emptied this section on the map." (`EMPTIED_SECTION_LINE`, for the gate's `EMPTIED_SECTION_REASON`; task 4.14b); and `weaveChanges`, the map's changes to the weave, each with its source, a meeting change named by the place the meeting names its line by, from the payload's `meetingChanges`, as "Your change to why it lands", never by an id the meeting does not show (`weaveChangeSource`; task 4.14b), with `changedBeside.weaveChanges` under them. `changedBeside` (`{gapNote, length, weaveChanges}`) holds the changed lines about those three places, which 4C's routing moves out of the round's lines; the board reads an absent field as empty (`changedBesideOf`).

After the parts come the note box with the standing notes folded under it, Approve and Send back (two clicks), the held line, and the trace of an automatic rework, folded: the board's only two `CollapsibleSection`s (R8).

**A card** is its `label`, the move's title, with the arrow that opens its `synopsis` (none on a map from before piece 4), its people, its `dots` (`{tone, name}` per thread, the name in the dot's `title`: a story thread's tone, the grey tone 0 with its name for a thread outside the story, 0 and no name for an id the weave does not hold), `cardMark` ("Card", `MAP_CARD_MARK`, from `beatCardOf`) and its `photos` as thumbnails. Under its title, selected or not, sits what the round says of it (spec 7): its `failures`, in the director's words, `concerns`, each read past its rule ids (`concernsBesideLines`, which the meeting reads too; brief 4.10d), and `changed`. Each card's summary toggle and the board's are this screen's own state, as are the selection and the thread picked, all reset when a new map version opens, so the page opens with every summary folded each time. Each card is keyed by its beat's id, so an editor open on it survives a strike or a move above it; a beat whose id another beat of its list holds, or with none, is keyed by its place (task 4.14b).

**Selecting** (spec 5). A card is a list item; its title is the button that selects it, named by `labels.select` ("Select the move "<title>""), with `aria-expanded`, and holds no other control. The card itself takes no role, because it holds other controls, which a button's role would make presentational (fix B3). A click on the card or its title, or Enter or Space on the title, selects it; clicking it again, Escape, or selecting something else closes it, and one thing is selected at a time. A click from a card's own controls or open parts (`INNER`) neither selects nor closes it. A selected card opens in place with its summary, its people, its threads by name, its photos with the director's descriptions, its controls (Edit the words, Move up and Move down, `canMoveUp` and `canMoveDown`, Move to another section, `moveTargets`, Put a photo beside it, `photoChoices`, and Leave it out, or Take it out for a move the director added at this look, `added`), and what's behind it under `view.evidenceTitle`: each piece of its `evidence` (`evidenceFoldView`, its sources named through the payload's `evidenceIndex`, a piece that cuts against the move marked so, the card's document marked `MAP_PRINTS_AS_CARD`, "Prints as a card", through `printsAsCard`), `meets`, where its threads meet, and `noEvidence`, `MAP_NO_EVIDENCE_LINE` ("Nothing yet: the article writer finds the evidence for it."), for a move the director added with none (the payload's `addedBeats`). A move in the tray opens the same way, with Bring it back into a section (`targets`). A photo is its thumbnail, from `/sessionphotos/<id>/<file>` (`mapPhotoUrl`), with the director's description (`photoDescriptionOf`, by `photoKey`) in its `title`, and in its place when the picture cannot load, else its filename (R7); selected (`labels.select`), it shows its description and where it can go: beside a move of its own section or by itself there (`besideOptions`), beside a move of any section (`besideTargets`), and to the top or into another section by itself (`moveTargets`). A photo the director has left out since (the payload's `leftOutPhotos`) shows `LEFT_OUT_PHOTO_LINE` ("You left this photo out of the article, so it does not print.") before its concerns, with no controls (task 4.14b). A line under an id the writer repeated, or a photo the writer placed twice, is locked: its controls are off and it does not drag.

**Drag and drop** is the browser's own (spec 6). A card or a photo drags with what it moves in its `dataTransfer`, as JSON under the board's own type (`DRAG_TYPE`), so a drop elsewhere on the page takes nothing. A card dropped on a card lands just before it, in its column or another, and one dropped on a column lands at its foot (`moveCard`, or `bringBack` from the tray), a card of that column too, which no card's drop takes to its last place, while the card already last there takes no landing; dropped in the tray it is left out, or taken out when the director added it, as its own button does (`leaveOut`, `takeOut`). A photo dropped on a move goes beside it, in any section (`placePhoto`); on a column it stands there by itself (`movePhotoTo`, or `photoBeside` with no move for a photo of that column); on the top of the article it becomes the top photo (`movePhotoTo`). Each drop target (`dropTarget`, with `landOnCard`, `landOnColumn`, `landInTray` and `landOnTop`, which say what a drop of the thing dragged runs, nothing for a drop the target does not take, which goes on to the column around a card, or `STAYS` for a drag already where it would land, the card itself or a photo over the move it sits beside, which the card stops there with no landing, so its column makes no move of it) shows where the drag will land while it is over it: a line above the card a move lands before, a dashed outline round the card a photo goes beside, or round the column, the tray or the top; the card dragged dims. Every move has one handler (`moveCard`, `moveCardBy`, `leaveOut`, `takeOut`, `bringBack`, `movePhotoTo`, `photoBeside`, `placePhoto`), the one call site of its `EditLogic` op, which its button and its drop both call (R10). Every drag can also be done through the selected card's controls, for the keyboard and a touch screen. The holds are under **Modifying Checkpoint Components**.

No line or label names a move, a thread or a connection by its id (`mapPathWords`, `mapEditLineOptions`, which name a move by its title), and every aria-label that names a move or a photo is `mapView`'s (`labels`, keyed by the control as the meeting's are: a move's controls by its title, its fold's through `evidenceFoldLabel`, a photo's controls by the director's description or its filename, each holding the words its control shows with the move or photo named in place of "it", as WCAG 2.5.3 asks: Leave it out is 'Leave "<title>" out'; fix B2), so `Outline.js` builds no label (brief 1F; fix round 3); the words its controls show (a selected card's, a tray move's and a photo's controls, the add line, the move's editor and the expected length's editor) are `MAP_CONTROLS`, which it prints as they are (fixes B1 and D1). In `lib/stop-pages.js` each synopsis (unless `synopsesOpen`), each card's threads, each thread's line, the evidence and each photo's description are folded lines (R7, R8). There is no evaluation bar, no `RevisionDiff`, no JSON editor and no writers' questions panel. The board's look is `console.css`'s "4.9: the map" section, in the console's tokens, each thread tone a token on `:root` (`--map-tone-0` to `--map-tone-8`). The map's stop lifts `.console-main`'s 960px cap to 1,520px, the room six columns at their narrowest need with the page's padding (`.console-main:has(.map)`; fix G2), so the board shows every column on a desktop screen, while every other stop keeps 960px.

- **Each line has an editor** in `outline-edit-logic.js`: the headline and the deck, the gap note's line, a section's heading and job, a move (its title, its summary and its people, the editor's three fields, opened from Edit the words on its selected card; brief 1D and piece 4: `initBeat`, `buildBeat`, which keep every other field of the beat, its evidence among them, as it is), and the expected length (a whole number; commas allowed).
- **The moves** (`outline-edit-logic.js`): a beat moved to another section, a photo beside it going with it; struck into left out, whole, its photos staying in their section by themselves; brought back from left out into a section the director picks, its photos beside it again (task 4.14b: a struck beat's photo keeps the beat's name on the director's map, every reader reads it as by itself, `photoBeatOf`, and the gate stores it so, `freeStruckBeatPhotos`, so a strike undone leaves the map as it was and records no edit); added as `{id, move, players}` under the next free `b<n>` (`addBeat`; brief 1D), or taken out again at the look that added it, its photos staying in their section by themselves, as a struck beat's do (4.6c), through the one helper that frees a beat's photos, which the gate's freeing of a strike's photos calls too (`freePhotosBeside`; 4.6d); a photo moved to another section, by itself, or to the top, where the top photo it replaces takes its place, so every photo stays placed once (no photo is struck on the map). Piece 4's moves with a place: `moveBeat(map, id, toSlot, index)` and `bringBackBeat(map, id, toSlot, index)` put the beat at `index` among the section's beats, at its foot with none (within its own section, no index or its own place leaves the map as it was); `moveBeatBy(map, id, delta)`, Move up (-1) and Move down (+1) within its section, leaves the map as it was at either end, in left out and for any other delta; `placePhotoBeside(map, fromSlot, fromIndex, toSlot, beatId)` puts a photo, the top photo too, beside a move of any section in one op, moving it into that section, and refuses a move the section does not hold. A line under an id the writer repeated, or a photo the writer placed twice, has its controls off. The page and the validator take a beat's id and card, where each beat and photo sits and what the map repeats from `outline-edit-logic.js`'s readers, so the lines the page locks sit under exactly the repeats `mapRepeats` finds in the map shown, which the validator takes as the writer's.
- **The counts** are built from the map as edited through `mapTally` (`mapTallyOf`), on the inputs the payload carries (task 4.6c), which sends no count of its own (4.6d): the roster with the canon's full names (`roster`) and the photos kept for the article (`keptPhotos`). So "In no move" lists the players in roster order after any edit, and a roster name counts whatever it is (`mapTally`'s set of players seen). The cards line says when they leave 3 to 5 (`MAP_CARDS`, held equal to `lib/map.js`'s).
- **The payloads are 4.6's** (`mapPayload(action, map, note)`): `{outline: 'approve' | 'send-back', map, note?}`, with the map on screen, edited or not; a send-back needs its note. Before either, `mapProblems` holds the map to the gate's decisions: `validateOutlineShape(map, theme, mapSlots, shown, leftOutPhotos)`, with the map shown read as `mapView` reads it (`isMapValue`, so a value that is no map is no map shown; 4.6c) and as the gate reads it (4.6d), through the helper both call (`shownMapOf`; 4.6e), is the director-side schema, then `lib/map.js` `directorMapProblems`'s rule for a repeat (a beat id the director's changes repeat; one the map shown holds is the writer's), and 4.6b's (a photo the director's changes place more than once), and 4.14b's (a photo the director left out, put at the top by their changes: `mapProblems` passes the payload's `leftOutPhotos` as `validateOutlineShape`'s fifth argument). `console/__tests__/checkpoint-view-logic-map.test.js` holds the decisions equal on one corpus, 4.6b's photo repeat included, and a shown value that is no map since 4.6e (4.6d tested it beside the corpus). `app.js`'s fallback approve is `mapPayload('approve', …)` too.
- **Pending edits.** Every change goes to the map's `pendingEdits` slot as `{version, map}` with the note beside it (`mapPendingSlot`), under the meeting's version rule (`stopVersion`: the map and both round counts). `CHECKPOINT_RECEIVED` keeps that slot and its note only while the stop shows the same version (`pendingEditsAfterCheckpoint`).
- **Thumbnails** load from `/sessionphotos/<id>/<file>` (`mapPhotoUrl`); a photo the console cannot serve shows the director's description in its place, or its filename when there is none.
