# The judges

The two truth-only judges (the story meeting's fact check and the article judge): the rule set they read, their truth criteria, what each sees, and the notes at the stops.

Moved verbatim from `reports/CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-11, when CLAUDE.md became a map. A part named in bold elsewhere in this text, such as **The map**, may now sit in another file in `docs/architecture/`; CLAUDE.md lists which file holds which part.

**The judges read the rule set** (phase 3, brief 3.4). Each judge's system prompt opens as a writer's does, through `systemPromptOpening(theme, call, sessionConfig)` (brief 4.13b): the theme's identity line for its call, the session's mode block, the world and the truth rules (`loadRuleSet('judge-arc' | 'judge-article', {theme})`), then its instructions; neither judge reads a craft file (`RULE_SET_CALLS['judge-arc']` and `RULE_SET_CALLS['judge-article']` are core only, the phase 4 spec's section 11; the article judge since brief 4.7a). Each judge scores the truth rules as structural **truth criteria** (`TRUTH_GROUPS`, `truth: true`, with `rules` and no weight): `evidenceTruth` (T1, T3, T4, T6), `moneyTruth` (T5), `verdictTruth` (T2), `stagesTruth` (T7), `novaPositionTruth` (T8), `playersTruth` (T9, T11), `wordsTruth` (T12), and for the article `photosTruth` (T13) and `fictionTruth` (T14); a weave places no photos and prints nothing. Each criterion asks only what its judge can check against its own prompts and what the judged output can hold, because a clause the judge cannot check scores low and sends the draft to a rework with nothing to fix: a group is worded per judge, and its `reads` names the material it checks by the heading `TRUTH_MATERIAL` gives, which the tests find in that judge's prompts. So only the article's `wordsTruth` asks about card text, and its `photosTruth` that it prints every photo in its PHOTOS and no other, each caption keeping the director's description. Since 3.9 the article writer's and judge's PHOTOS is every photo the director kept, so T13's every-photo check is the article's (the integrator's ruling). `moneyTruth` (3.9; T5, R11) asks whether the money runs from the buyer to the seller's chosen account, with NeurAI and its board written as Nova's suspicion of who the buyer is, and names each figure's source (the final fix): each sale, the first-burial bonus and each transfer as the ledger gives it; at the article judge each total at the close of the investigation as the writers' FINANCIAL_SUMMARY gives it; and a balance the director's notes record as said or shown in the room as that moment's figure (T1), so a judge never corrects a player's line to a closing total. Since phases 14 and 15 (brief F; R9, spec 11) the questions follow the approved rules at both judges: `evidenceTruth` asks for a direction a note at a stop gives written as Nova's reading (its clause on character sheets went on 2026-10-10, when the director ruled a sheet never in the record), what the record cannot back as Nova's suspicion, allegation, question or opinion (T1), and the character an account is named after only wondered about, or read as the target of a frame, in Nova's opinion or question (T4); `moneyTruth` takes the ledger as the investigation's payments, and a ledger line a note at a stop explains as the note gives it; `stagesTruth` asks for the investigation at the time of day the session's facts give, morning or afternoon, Nova's day taken from the epilogue and from any note at a stop that adds to it, and each logged time as the record's timeline prints it or a note at a stop rules it, and reads `stopNotes` (a note at any stop corrects a shift for the judges too, spec 4); `novaPositionTruth` asks whether Nova takes no side in the verdict, with where Nova stood and what part Nova took in the room as the mode block states them (T8); and `fictionTruth` asks for no rule of the game explained to the players beside no production word (T14). Every weave question that says where the director's words come from names the notes at the stops beside the notes and the answers, and so does each `verdictTruth`. Since brief 4.7a the article's questions follow section B of the rule-text read (T1, T2, T5 and T9 as rewritten), each pinned in `lib/__tests__/article-judge.test.js`: `verdictTruth` asks for every theory the room debated that a beat in the map's sections carries, reported, and every theory in the map's `leftOut`, where a beat the director struck sits, kept out of print, and since brief 4.7f reads the map as the director's desk edits leave it, the edits first: a theory the director cut, or took out in a rewrite, is out of the map's sections, and one their own text reports is in them, from `leftOut` too, so a theory whose card they cut is not read as unreported, which a finding quoting neither the cut text nor the edit's id would have kept structural (`TRUTH_MATERIAL` names `directorEdits`, the section the judge reads them under); `playersTruth` for the roster's pronoun, or the one the director's own words give, or else the player's name; `moneyTruth` for a figure raised as a question at the story meeting as the director's answer gives it, and out of print when the question has no answer; and the director's answers join the `reads` of `evidenceTruth`, `moneyTruth`, `playersTruth` and `verdictTruth` (`TRUTH_MATERIAL` names `answers`, `weave` and `map`). An article question that names where the director's words come from (`evidenceTruth`, `moneyTruth`, `playersTruth`, `wordsTruth`) names the sources the fact check reads as the director's words (`buildFactCheckArgs`' `directorWords`): the notes, the input-review corrections, the accusation, the answers at the story meeting and the notes at the stops (`ARTICLE_DIRECTOR_WORDS`), and reads them all (`DIRECTOR_WORDS_MATERIAL`; `TRUTH_MATERIAL` names `corrections` and `stopNotes`); the weave's questions keep their wording, and every question at either judge that reads the director's words reads `stopNotes` (by hand on the weave's and on `verdictTruth`). `evidenceTruth`'s T6 clause reads an exposer from the same sources (brief 4.7d), because a correction, an answer or a note that names who turned a memory in is the director's words (T1). The sources are one list, `lib/director-words.js` `DIRECTOR_WORDS_SOURCES` (phases 14 and 15, brief B; R1: each source's `key`, `label`, `material` and `texts(state)`, the notes at the stops read from `directorGateNotes` whole, never through `filterGateNotes`, so a judge reads the note a rework acted on too), with `directorWordsTexts(state)` every source's texts in that order. `evaluator-nodes.js` builds `ARTICLE_DIRECTOR_WORDS`, `DIRECTOR_WORDS_MATERIAL` and `directorWords` from it, so the questions, the judge's reads, the fact check's `directorText` and the verdict guard's record (`recordTexts`) name the same sources in one order, and `lib/evidence.js` `notesTextsOf` reads it, so a piece of evidence may quote a note at a stop under `"notes"`, whose gloss (`SOURCES_GLOSS`) names "the notes at the stops". "From your notes" at the story meeting is not among its readers: `arc-specialist-nodes.js` `directorWordsOf` keeps the session notes and the corrections. A breach is written as a structural issue that opens with its rule ids and quotes the text at fault beside the record it contradicts, and `createEvaluator` holds an output whose truth criterion scored below `STRUCTURAL_PASS_SCORE` (0.8, defined in `node-helpers.js` since 3.10) to not-ready whatever the judge's `structuralPassed` says, adding the criterion's notes under its rule ids when the judge wrote no issue for it (`failedTruthCriteria`, `truthIssueLines`), or, when the judge gave it no notes and no fix, a plain sentence naming what the criterion checks, never its key (its group's `about`, `unexplainedTruthFault`; brief 4.10d: "T5: The judge found a fault in the money and gave no detail."), so it is among the must-fix, labelled, and one left at the cap is marked at the desk (task 4.10). A truth criterion the judge leaves out of its scores counts as not scored: it is logged by name (`unscoredTruthCriteria`, "Truth criteria not scored: …") and does not hold the output. Neither judge writes a craft finding since phase 4 (briefs 4.4 and 4.7a): no note on the writing reaches a stop, and nothing in code holds a draft on an advisory. The article judge's weighted criteria (`voiceConsistency`, `antiPatterns`, `reporterMode`, `arcThreading` and the four advisory ones), its craft-findings section, its "MUST be actionable" block and its frame (the immutable inputs, the compelling gift) went in brief 4.7a (spec 6.2): T8 as the mode block states it is `novaPositionTruth`'s, T14 `fictionTruth`'s, and C4's em-dash house rule and the length are the fact check's advisories. **The story meeting's fact check** (phase 4, brief 4.4; spec 4.5) is the arc stage's judge. It scores the truth criteria alone (`getArcCriteria()`, one set for every theme), each worded for the weave: `verdictTruth` asks for the verdict told as the room's official story, ungraded against any hidden answer, with the theories the room debated left to the map (T2 as rewritten), and a breach names the thread or connection it is in and, where one is at fault, the piece of evidence by its sources, so the fix can cite something else (`TRUTH_FINDING_QUOTE`; phase 4b, brief 1B). Its prompt opens by naming the evidence under each line, and it reads each line against its evidence and each piece against the record. It reads the scoring rules a truth-only judge reads (`TRUTH_ONLY_EVALUATION_RULES`: the truth criteria are the whole evaluation, with no weighted average and no advisory criteria, and `overallScore` is the lowest truth-criterion score; the article judge reads them too since brief 4.7a), and the director's notes under the arc writer's own label (`ARC_NOTES_LABEL`, imported from `arc-specialist-nodes.js`, so the two read the notes under one text). It writes to the truth-only output contract (fix round 1; `truthOnlyOutputFormat` in its prompt, `TRUTH_ONLY_EVALUATION_JSON_SCHEMA` for the SDK): no criterion type, and `advisoryWarnings` only for a concern about one of the director's edits, empty otherwise (`TRUTH_ONLY_ADVISORY_WARNINGS`, one wording for both). `createEvaluator` holds the verdict to that contract after the verdict guard (`truthOnlyVerdict`): each criterion it was given keeps its definition's type, so the automatic fix never reads a breach's fix as a suggestion; a criterion it was not given, and each advisory that is not a concern under `DIRECTOR_EDIT_PREFIX`, are left out and logged. So no note on the writing reaches the meeting or the fix. Its readiness follows from the contract alone (brief 4.5, ruling 8): ready when no structural issue remains and no truth criterion holds the output, whatever the judge's own `structuralPassed`, with the director's edits standing or without, so a breach the judge lists gets its fix though the judge called the weave passed, and a criterion outside the contract holds nothing. The arc judge's weighted criteria, its NPC and roster lists, its checklist and its craft findings went. **The article judge** (brief 4.7a; spec 6.2) is truth-only too: one judge for every theme (R1), whose identity line says it checks the article the article writer wrote before the director reads it, with the truth-only rules and OUTPUT FORMAT (its notes name the sentence at fault, its section and the record it contradicts), and the truth-only schema and verdict (`truthOnlyVerdict`). Since brief 4.7c the truth-only contract is the judges' one contract: the weighted schema, the weighted OUTPUT FORMAT and the `isTruthOnly` seam that chose between them went with their last caller, and `createEvaluator` sends every judge `TRUTH_ONLY_EVALUATION_JSON_SCHEMA` and holds every verdict to it. An `issues` array lies outside that contract, so one a verdict carries anyway is logged and read nowhere: not in the history entry (which holds no `issues` key since task 4.12c: the alias that repeated its structural issues went with its last reader, the e2e harness, which reads `structuralIssues` since task 4.12a), not in `validationResults`, not in the escalation reason. So it is ready when no structural issue remains and no truth criterion holds, an advisory that is no concern about the director's edits reaches neither the stop nor the rework, and its escalation at the cap lists its breaches and its concerns, and no note on the writing (`guard.advisories`). `overallScore` stays in its contract; 4.10 drops its display. The article judge reads only the bundle's printed fields (`printedBundle`: never `voice_self_check`, pull quotes, the top-level photos, a sidebar entry's content or owner, or the writer's tracker when the page prints the ledger's; when no `state.shellAccounts` account has a positive total the page prints the writer's tracker, so the judge reads its entries' descriptions and amounts and its total). Criteria resolve through `getPhaseCriteria(phase, theme)`. **The output contract** (3.9; `TRUTH_ONLY_EVALUATION_JSON_SCHEMA` and `truthOnlyOutputFormat` since brief 4.7c) asks for a criterion's `fix` only when it scores below `STRUCTURAL_PASS_SCORE`, and for `revisionGuidance` only as the steps that fix the structural issues, one per issue; verdicts stored before it still carry fixes on passing criteria, which the rework context drops (see **The rework rules**). **A replay does not re-pay an evaluation** (3.9): `evaluatePhase` skips a phase whose most recent entry is ready or escalated to the director (`escalatedToHuman`), so a replay from START reaches an escalated stop without a new evaluation; every article rework appends a not-ready stub first (`incrementArticleRevision` in `graph.js`), and a rollback appends one or clears the history (`buildRollbackState`), so a changed output is still evaluated. The story meeting's fact check skips by its mark on the weave instead (see **Arc Validation Routing**). The detective's judges went with its old stages (R1): its arc judge with the arc stage (brief 4.4), its outline judge with the outline stage (brief 4.6), and its article judge, with its criteria, its prompts and its pinned render, in brief 4.7a; a detective state gets the one article judge.

**What the judges see** (phase 2, brief 2.4). `evaluator-nodes.js#buildEvaluationUserPrompt(phase, state, {factCheck})` gives each Opus judge the record and its writer's inputs through the writers' own renderers and builders, never a private cut. Each gets the roster with pronouns (`PromptBuilder#_rosterSection`, through `getPromptBuilder`: the section the article writer's system prompt carries, without pronouns on a detective session; since the final fix wave an NPC is listed once, in its NPC block, a canonical character not on the session roster gets no pronoun, and a roster character with none captured keeps they/them; since phases 14 and 15 (R4) Nova's NPC line also says Nova signs the article with the session's first name and the director's notes may call Nova by it, `lib/prompt-renderers/session-authors.js` `writerSignsLine`, from the theme's Nova entry, `writer.signs`, and `sessionConfig.journalistFirstName`, else `writer.defaultFirstName`, so every writer, rework and judge reads it in both modes), the director's notes with the input-review corrections after them (`renderDirectorEnrichmentBlock`, which appends `renderDirectorCorrectionsBlock`), and the director's accusation word for word beside its parse (roadmap 2.4):
- **Arc judge** (the story meeting's fact check since phase 4, brief 4.4): the weave as the writer returned it (`weaveForPrompt`, without the fact check's mark), the accusation as the arc writer renders it (`renderArcAccusation(playerFocus.accusation, directorAccusationText(state), "Players' Reasoning")`), the guest reporter's line when the session has one (`guestReporterLine`; R5), the roster section, the director's notes under the arc writer's label (`renderJudgeDirectorNotes(state, ARC_NOTES_LABEL)`), the director's answers and the notes at the stops (below), and the whole record view with the session config (`renderRecordView(bundle, {sessionConfig})`), its sales on the investigation's timeline under the view's one row rule (`isBuriedTransactionRow`), and when the investigation ran (`timeOfDayLine`, before the guest reporter's line and the roster with pronouns; phases 14 and 15, brief E, R7), since it reads no SESSION_FACTS. The code checks hold the evidence's sources and the verdict thread, so it reads no valid-id list, no coverage roster and no PLAYER FOCUS.
- **Article judge** (the outline judge, which read the same session context, left in phase 4, brief 4.6): `renderJudgeSessionContext`, the time-of-day line (the facts' `timeOfDayLine`; brief E, R7) then the session roster (`buildSessionFacts`) with the guest reporter's line after it when the session has one (`renderJudgeSessionRoster`, from the facts' `guestReporterLine`; R5), the roster section, the verdict (`renderSessionFactsVerdict`: the parsed accusation, the director's account word for word, the whiteboard) and the notes; the whole record view, then, since 3.9, the writers' FINANCIAL_SUMMARY (`renderJudgeFinancialSummary`: the writers' own builder, `_buildFinancialSummary`, over the same `state.shellAccounts`, whose total line calls the sum what the buyer paid out during the investigation and names no payer, T5; "this morning" until phases 14 and 15, brief E, R8), which `moneyTruth` reads; since phase 3 (3.4) the photos the article writer was given (`renderArticleJudgePhotos`, built since 3.9 from the writer's own inputs, `articleWriterInputs`: the hero, then every other photo the director kept, without the whiteboard, each once as the writer's `renderPhotoEntry` line with the director's description), which `photosTruth` holds the article to; since brief 4.7a, the director's answers at the story meeting right after the notes (`renderDirectorAnswers`, as the meeting's fact check reads them), then the notes at the stops (below), and, between PHOTOS and the bundle, the writer's task: the settled weave (`settledWeaveOf`, every question with the director's answer or the mark that it is unanswered) and the map as the director left it (`state.outline`, under its own `MAP:` line, the beats the director struck in its `leftOut`); and, after the bundle and the director's edits, the fact check's result for the bundle under review as two lists under a plain label (structural issues, advisories), nothing else of the object. `createEvaluator` passes the result it has just computed; the state's `_articleFactCheck` is never read, because until this evaluation writes it, it belongs to the previous bundle.

**The notes at the stops** (phases 14 and 15, brief B; spec 2026-10-09 section 3; R2). Both judges print every note the director has sent at a stop in `<DIRECTOR_STOP_NOTES>` (`TRUTH_MATERIAL.stopNotes`), right after `<DIRECTOR_ANSWERS>` (right after the notes when no question is answered): `lib/prompt-renderers/director-words-renderer.js` `renderDirectorStopNotes(state)`, which opens with one line pointing at T1, then gives each note in the order filed, under its stop as the console names it in words (the story meeting, the map, the article) and the action it went with ("approved" or "sent back": a reweave's note is filed as a rejection, as a send-back's is, so both read as sent back), its text word for word, and holds "None." when there is none, so every question that reads it finds it. It reads `directorGateNotes` whole (`lib/director-words.js` `stopNotesOf`), from state, so `scripts/lib/render-calls.js` passes nothing new.

At the article the fact check still runs first and short-circuits the judge's call under the automated budget. (Brief 2.4's measure, on the 092026 thread: the arc, outline and article judges' user prompts grew from 71.5K, 69.8K and 68.5K characters to 137K, 184K and 154K.) Both judges score the truth criteria alone since phase 4 (see **The judges read the rule set**), and no stop shows a model score since task 4.10: see the `lastEvaluation` row under "Checkpoint payload keys the gates read".
