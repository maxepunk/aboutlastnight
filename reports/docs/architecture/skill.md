## Journalist Skill (Direct Claude Code Usage)

**Location:** `.claude/skills/journalist-report/`
**Purpose:** Article generation via Claude Code without server

**Custom Subagents:** image-analyzer, evidence-curator, arc-analyzer, outline-generator, article-generator, article-validator (`.claude/agents/journalist-*.md`), exactly the six SKILL.md starts. Each definition holds its job, inputs and outputs and lists the rule files it reads, the files its pipeline counterpart's call reads (`RULE_SET_CALLS`); every writing rule is the rule set's. The three specialists (financial, behavioral, victimization), which nothing started, were retired in phase 3.
**The stages** (phase 4, brief 4.12b; R13): the skill follows the pipeline's. The arc analyzer pitches the weave (`analysis/weave.json`), two or three angles over one set of threads (phase 4b, piece 3), which the director settles at the story meeting in chat: they pick an angle (`picked`), rewrite its pitch or a thread's words, flip threads in or out of it and add one, each change written into the weave and listed in `directorChanges`, answer the questions, and approve, reweave (a rework of the open angle alone, which keeps every other angle as it was) or send it back. The outline generator lays the settled angle across the theme's slots as the story map (`analysis/article-outline.json`, in the shape of `lib/schemas/outline.schema.json`), each section's moves in the order the article tells them, each with its summary (`synopsis`), under both of the map's bounds (phase 4b, piece 4). The director settles it at the map's stop in chat, shown as the console's corkboard shows it: the counts, the settled story and its threads, then each section's moves in order, each by its title, its people, its threads and "Card" where it prints as a card, its summary shown when the director asks; they edit any line, a summary among them, reorder a section's moves, move, leave out or bring back a move, and place the photos. The article generator writes from both, each section's moves in the map's order, each as its summary says, where it has one. The validator is the article judge's counterpart (the `judge-article` call): must-fix findings under the truth rules and for a player the map places whom the page never names, two flags (an em-dash in the narrator's prose, the prose over 1,800 words), and no notes on the writing. The skill's deferred items (R13: whiteboard name matching, a folder for its inputs, the ledger and the clock in code, the agents' models) wait for the director's decision.
**Reference Files:** the rule set in `references/rules/` (`world.md`, `truth-rules.md`, the `craft-*.md` files and the two mode files), and `references/schemas.md`, the shapes of the files the steps pass to one another (the map and the bundle take theirs from `lib/schemas/`, the map's slots from `lib/theme-config.js`). `__tests__/unit/journalist-skill-path.test.js` holds SKILL.md, schemas.md and the agents to the rule files' lint, the agents folder to the agents SKILL.md starts, each agent's inputs and outputs to the stages, the weave's shape and kinds in schemas.md to `WEAVE_SCHEMA`, `lib/weave.js` and `lib/writer-questions.js`, and the skill path to the angles: no role, main thread or strike on a connection; `lib/__tests__/retired-craft-files.test.js` holds each agent's rule list to its call in `RULE_SET_CALLS`.

**Rendering:** Article generation emits a `ContentBundle` JSON and renders HTML via
`scripts/assemble-article.js`, which uses the shared `TemplateAssembler` (same as
the server pipeline). Output is structurally identical across both paths.
